## 7. Karma (reliability system)

7.1 **Karma measures reliability, not skill.** It rewards showing up on time, paying, good
conduct and successful referrals, and penalises behaviour that disrupts Games for others.

7.2 Examples of how Karma changes (values may be adjusted over time):
   - **+** playing on time, punctuality streaks, good-conduct awards;
   - **+20** when a friend you referred is approved;
   - **–** late/very late cancellations, no-shows, late arrivals, non-payment and
     misconduct.

7.3 **Standing tiers.** Your Karma balance affects your access:
   - a healthy balance keeps full access;
   - a **low balance restricts** your ability to sign up to Games;
   - reaching **zero suspends** sign-ups until your standing recovers.

7.4 Penalties may **decay** over time, and Admins may make **manual corrections** (for
example, to reverse a penalty applied in error). Karma is an internal community mechanic and
has no monetary value.
