## 4. Why we process your data and our legal basis

Under the DIFC DP Law, processing must have a lawful basis. For membership applications and
messaging we rely primarily on **consent** and on the **performance of a contract** (or
steps taken at your request before entering into membership / Game arrangements).

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---------|-------------|
| Review your application / invitation and decide on membership | Consent; steps at your request prior to a contract |
| Operate your membership and organise Games (registration, waitlist, partners, confirmations, results) | Performance of a contract |
| Run the Karma reliability system and leaderboards | Performance of a contract; legitimate interests in running a fair Community |
| Send **service** messages via WhatsApp (reminders, confirmations, announcements) | Consent (and to deliver the Service) |
| Send **marketing** messages and partner offers via WhatsApp | Consent (separate, optional — you can withdraw anytime) |
| Handle payments and fee collection (incl. late-cancellation/no-show fees) | Performance of a contract; legal obligation |
| Provide support and respond to your requests | Consent; performance of a contract |
| Keep the Community safe (conduct, bans, fraud/abuse prevention) | Legitimate interests; legal obligation |
| Keep records to prove consent and comply with law | Legal obligation |

You can **withdraw consent** at any time (see Section 9). Withdrawal does not affect
processing already carried out, and some processing may continue on another legal basis
(for example, to complete a Game you joined or to keep required records).

## 5. WhatsApp and international transfers

The Service uses **WhatsApp (Meta)** as its primary messaging channel. When we send you
service or marketing messages, the necessary data (such as your phone number and message
content) is processed by Meta on infrastructure **outside the DIFC / UAE**. This is an
international transfer of your personal data.

By opting in to WhatsApp messaging you **consent to this transfer** for the purpose of
delivering the Service, and we put in place appropriate safeguards as required by the DIFC
DP Law. Meta processes this data under its own terms. You can stop service or marketing
messages at any time (for example, by adjusting your preferences in the app or replying
**STOP**).

## 6. Who we share your data with

- **Organisers/Admins** of Padel Nomads, to run the Community and Games.
- **Other players**, on a limited basis needed to play — for example, your name, level,
  roster photo, and, for confirmed Games, coordination details with your partner/team.
- **Clubs/Venues**, where needed to host a Game.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf (for example, messaging
  infrastructure and, in future, payment providers), under appropriate processor
  obligations.
- **Authorities**, where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do **not** sell your personal data. Partner offers are delivered **by us**; we do not
hand your contact details to partners unless you choose to redeem an offer directly with
them.

## 7. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above:

- **Active members:** for the duration of your membership.
- **After you leave / are removed:** for a limited period afterwards to handle disputes,
  settle fees, meet legal obligations and prevent abuse, after which we delete or
  anonymise it.
- **Consent and safety records** (e.g. ban records) may be kept longer where needed to
  comply with law or protect the Community.

A detailed retention schedule is maintained internally and available on request.

## 8. How we protect your data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures — including access controls,
encryption in transit, and restricted staff access — and maintain records of our processing
activities. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify
you and the **DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection** of a qualifying personal-data breach
as required by the DIFC DP Law.

## 9. Your rights under the DIFC DP Law

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in the DIFC DP Law, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and information about how it is processed;
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erase** your data in certain cases;
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it;
- **Object to direct marketing** at any time;
- **Data portability** — receive certain data in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **Object to solely automated decisions** that significantly affect you.

To exercise any right, contact **[DPO / PRIVACY EMAIL]** or use the controls in your
profile (for example, WhatsApp preferences and "manage my data"). We will respond within
the timeframes required by law. You will not be charged for making a request.
